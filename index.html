AMC Pathway

For IMGs seeking general or specialist registration with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA) via the Standard Pathway.

    Why Guiding Hand

    Coaching built around your exam, not a textbook.

    Pathway-specific roadmaps

    A sequencing plan built around your target country, current exam status, and realistic timeline — not a fixed course calendar.

    Blueprint-mapped question banks

    Mock exams patterned on each test's actual structure — AMC CAT MCQ, USMLE CK-style vignettes, PLAB 1 MCQs, PLAB 2 circuits.

    Mentors who've walked the route

    One-to-one guidance from doctors who completed the same licensing pathway you're preparing for.

    Registration & paperwork support

    Help navigating ECFMG, GMC, AHPRA and counselling documentation — the part no question bank prepares you for.

    How it works

    Five steps from first call to registration.

    01

    Pathway assessment

    03

    Structured prep
